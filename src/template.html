Research notes · 10 November 2026

Know the rules.

95 gods · Nox is the newest

The SMITE 2 Wiki list and SMITE 2 Codex both contain the same 95 names. SmiteSource release order lists Nox, OB47, released 3 November 2026. The Hi-Rez-authored OB47 notes (mirror) were published 2 November. Names, pantheons, primary attack damage and preferred roles are from the wiki; approximate classes and most epithets are from Codex. Strength and Intelligence are not equivalent to damage type. These labels are filters, not hard role restrictions.

Draft order

O = Order; C = Chaos. Standard: bans O–C–O–C–O–C; picks O–C–C–O–O–C–C–O–O–C. Extended: the same opening bans; picks O–C–C–O–O–C; bans C–O–C–O; picks C–O–O–C. These sequences are cross-checked against SMITE 2 Companion's public draft configuration. SMITE Prime 2026 Kick-Off specifies Draft Three Ban. There is no single claimed universal 2026 pro ruleset here.

Timers, locks and series

40s pick / 18s ban / 90s shared team bank follows the Companion Babylon community preset; configurable, not independently verified official timers. Bank starts after action time expires. All picks and bans are unique within this simulator; tournament duplicate-ban forfeits are intentionally prevented. Local undo and timeout pass are training conveniences.

SmiteSource Open 2026 rules say locked means final; repeat the same draft after disconnect; previous game's loser chooses next side; Fearless means previously played gods cannot be picked by either side again, while bans reset. Series scoring, Fearless carry-over, Aspects and staff-disabled god lists are not implemented in this single-game prototype.

Footage evidence

No footage was supplied. The fetched transcript of the January 2025 Founders Series Grand Finals explicitly describes two bans per team. This is historical evidence that formats changed, not the current preset. The indexed transcript for SMITE Prime 2026 Kick-Off: NA JOUST explains that played gods enter the vault and bans do not persist. This is Joust, not proof of Conquest action order. Transcripts were reviewed; no frame-by-frame video review was performed.

Online rooms

Same-screen play stays on this page. Online play uses a small WebSocket room server: the server holds the draft, Order is the host, Chaos is the guest, and a captain can only act on their own turn. Share the room code or the join link — both players just open this site. Rooms live in server memory, so a free host can forget them after it sleeps; a dropped tab can reconnect while the room is still awake. No accounts. Not for official tournaments.
